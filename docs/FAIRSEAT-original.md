# Fairseat

Buy and sell tickets from anyone, safely.

Ticketmaster protects you inside Ticketmaster. Fairseat protects you everywhere.

## The problem

- Fans buying tickets from strangers (Instagram, Facebook Marketplace, group chats) get scammed constantly. They pay, the seller disappears.
- Sellers get burned too: buyers claim they never received the ticket.
- Big resale platforms only protect you inside their own app, hold sellers' money until after the event, and charge high fees.
- Scalpers resell at many times face value.

## The idea

Every ticket is a digital asset the fan actually owns. When a ticket is sold, the payment and the ticket swap at the exact same moment. Either both happen or neither does, so neither side can scam the other.

AI handles the messy parts: spotting fake events, helping fans buy by voice, and protecting tickets for games that might not happen.

## Example: Game 7

1. **Organizer lists the game.** Chase Center pastes its official event link. The app verifies it's the real venue and creates tickets with a resale cap.
2. **Fan buys a ticket.** Payment and ticket swap instantly.
3. **Scammer gets blocked.** A fake listing from an unknown website is rejected before anyone pays.
4. **Scalping gets blocked.** A resale above the cap is refused. A fair resale goes through, and the organizer earns a small cut automatically.
5. **Buying by voice.** "Find me a Game 7 ticket under $200." The agent finds one, reads it out, and buys it when you say yes.
6. **Ticket protection.** Game 7 only happens if the series goes 3-3. For a small fee, the app buys a prediction market position that pays out if the game is cancelled.
7. **Game day.** The gate scans a QR that checks who owns the ticket right now. Old screenshots don't work.

## How it's different from normal ticket apps

- Not locked to one platform. You own the ticket, so you can safely buy from anyone, anywhere.
- Sellers are paid instantly, not after the event.
- Lower fees, since no middleman has to cover fraud risk.
- Rules enforced by code, not company policy.
- Ticket protection through prediction markets, which no ticket app offers.

## Known limits

- Buyers and sellers can still pay extra on the side to get around the price cap.
- Fans don't care about "blockchain," so the product never mentions it. It just feels safe and fast.
